Cards are listed once per printing, so a card reprinted as an illustration rare appears each time. Switch to One per name for a deck-builder view: a name counts as owned once you own any legal printing of it.

The G-mark rule applies only to Trainers and Energy. A G-mark Pokémon is never legal, even when a newer printing has the same text. Basic Energy from any era is legal; only Scarlet & Violet and Mega Evolution prints are listed here.

Card data: Pokémon TCG API, pulled October 27, 2026, through 30th Celebration. Rotation rules from the official announcement. Some promos from Pokémon Center boxes and the Mega Evolution promo line may be missing from the source data. Check the card’s own regulation mark when in doubt.
